# Security policy

Thanks for helping keep Azoth Vault and its visitors safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **contact@azothvault.com** with:

- what you found and where (page, URL or endpoint),
- steps to reproduce it,
- what someone could do with it.

Please don't open a public GitHub issue for security problems, and don't test against other people's accounts or data.

You can expect a reply within 7 days. Once a fix is live, we're happy to credit you here if you'd like.

## In scope

- https://azothvault.com and www.azothvault.com
- The site's Discord sign-in, saved loadouts, comments and ratings

## Out of scope

- New World itself, Amazon Games, Discord or Cloudflare (report those to them)
- Denial of service, spam or social engineering
