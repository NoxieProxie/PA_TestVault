New World Vault

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

This policy covers the New World Vault website (azothvault.com) and its Discord app and bot, called “the vault” below. The vault is a free fan project run by one person. It is not a company, and it is not run by or connected to Amazon Games or Discord.

The short version: you can read the whole site without logging in, and nothing about you is collected. If you log in with Discord, the vault learns your Discord ID, username, display name and avatar, and nothing else from your account. Your favourites are stored against your Discord ID. Nothing is sold or used for advertising, and there are no trackers or adverts.

What the website collects

WhatWhen, and why
Your Discord ID, username, display name and avatarOnly if you press “Log in with Discord”. The vault asks Discord for the identify permission only, which does not include your email address, servers, friends or messages. These details are put into a signed login pass that stays in your own browser for 30 days. Apart from the activity log below, they are not kept on the server.
Your favouritesOnly if you use Favourites, which needs a Discord login. The folders, the things in them, their names, descriptions, icons and your notes are stored in Cloudflare's key-value storage under your Discord ID, so they show on any device you log in from. Only you can read or change them.
Your saved loadoutsIf you're logged in with Discord, the loadouts you save under “Your loadouts” are stored in Cloudflare's key-value storage under your Discord ID, so they show on any device you log in from. Only you can read or change them. Without logging in they stay in your browser only.
Comments and ratingsOnly if you post a comment or rate a build or community loadout, which needs a Discord login. Comments are public: they show your Discord display name and profile picture, the text you wrote and when. Ratings are shown only as an average; the site stores your Discord ID with your star rating so you can change it. Both are kept in Cloudflare's D1 database. If you report a comment, your Discord ID is stored with the report for the site owner.
Loadouts you submitOnly if you press Submit on a loadout. It goes to the site owner for review as a GitHub issue that includes the loadout, its name and mode, and your Discord username and ID. The site's GitHub repository is public, so treat a submission as public. If the owner publishes it, it appears on the site under your Discord display name.
Ideas and problem reportsOnly if you send one from “Ideas and problems”, which needs a Discord login. What you write, the page you were on (if you leave that ticked) and your Discord ID, username and display name are stored in Cloudflare's key-value storage for the site owner. They're also posted to a private channel in the owner's Discord server. They're kept until the owner deletes them.
Editor updatesOnly for people the owner has made an editor. Updates you suggest, with your Discord ID, username and display name, are stored in Cloudflare's key-value storage until the owner reviews them, and a notice goes to the owner's private Discord channel. Approved updates are saved into the site's GitHub repository with your display name, and appear on the site.
Activity log (security)To keep the site safe, the helper records each login and each change made by the site owner or an editor: the time, the Discord ID and display name, and what was done (for example “suggested a change to Builds › Assassin”). Only the site owner can see it. It keeps the latest 500 entries and older ones drop off. It doesn't record what you read or which pages you visit.
Blocked accountsIf an account is used to spam or abuse the site's forms, the owner can block it. The block list holds that Discord ID, the name the owner gave it, an optional reason and the date. A blocked account can still read the site.
Settings saved in your browserPinned and recent pages, your saved loadouts, calculator and filter settings, and a copy of your favourites are kept in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them. This never leaves your device, except your favourites as described above. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.
Basic request logsCloudflare, which hosts the site and its helper, records technical details of requests (such as IP address, time and the page or address requested), as any web host does. The site owner can see these for a short time to fix problems. The site itself sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics.

What the Discord bot handles

The bot only works in servers that have added it. To run slash commands and the moderation features a server turns on, it handles:

MessagesThe bot reads new messages in the channels it can see, to spot spam, repeated messages and links the server has chosen to block. It keeps up to the last 800 messages in memory only, so moderators can see what an edited or deleted message said in the server's mod-log channel. These are lost whenever the bot restarts and are never written to storage.
MembersWhen someone joins, the bot can check how old their Discord account is (if the server turned this on), post a welcome message and give roles from role menus.
Moderation actionsIf the server's rules are broken, the bot may delete messages, time out, kick or ban, and posts a note about it in the server's mod-log channel. These notes stay in that Discord server, controlled by its moderators.
CommandsWhen you use a command like /build or /boss, Discord sends the vault the command and who used it, so it can reply.

The bot's settings for a server (for example which channel is the mod-log) are stored in the site's GitHub repository. They contain channel and role IDs, not messages.

Who else handles the data

The vault uses three services to run, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Discord: logging in, the bot and messages (discord.com/privacy).
  • Cloudflare: hosting the website, the helper that handles logins and favourites, the storage for favourites and saved loadouts, and the database for comments and ratings (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy).
  • GitHub: storing the site's files, the bot's settings and submitted loadouts (GitHub privacy statement).

Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

How long it's kept

  • Your login pass expires after 30 days, or straight away when you press Log out.
  • Favourites are kept until you delete them, either one by one or all at once with “Delete all my favourites” at the bottom of the Favourites page. New World's servers close on 31 January 2027; the vault may close some time after that, and stored favourites will then be deleted.
  • Submitted loadouts stay in the GitHub repository until the owner removes them.
  • Ideas and problem reports are kept until the owner deletes them; you can ask for yours to be removed.
  • Saved loadouts in your account are kept until you delete them.
  • Comments stay until you delete them (there's a Delete button on your own comments) or the site owner removes them. You can remove your rating at any time by clicking the same star again.
  • The activity log keeps the latest 500 entries; older ones are removed automatically, and the owner can clear it at any time. A block stays until the owner removes it.
  • The bot's message memory only lasts until it restarts.

Your choices

  • You can use the whole site without logging in.
  • You can delete your favourites at any time from the Favourites page.
  • You can ask the site owner to remove a loadout you submitted, or anything else held about you, by contacting them as described below.
  • You can remove the vault's access to your Discord account at any time in Discord: User Settings → Authorized Apps.

If you live in the UK or EU, you also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission).

Children

The vault is for people old enough to use Discord under Discord's own rules, which means at least 13, or older where local law requires it.
